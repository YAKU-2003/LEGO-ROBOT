What the grid sees

Hand mask: pixels redder than the background (skin) light up.

12 × 12 grid: how much of each cell is covered by hand — 144 numbers.

Finger profile: how high the hand reaches in each of 24 columns — raised fingers are the tall bumps.

These 168 numbers join the CNN's 9,216 learned features in the same final layer — one model.
